# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** February 13, 2026
**Last Updated:** February 13, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Broski, operated by humblemuzzu ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Broski mobile application ("App") and related services. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Broski is a mobile application that allows you to remotely control AI coding agents running on your personal computer from your mobile device. The App connects to a bridge server you run on your own machine and to our cloud services for authentication and subscription management.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect the following information through Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In:

| Data | Source | Purpose |
|------|--------|---------|
| Email address | Google or Apple sign-in | Account identification, subscription management, support communications |
| Display name | Google or Apple sign-in (if provided) | Account display |
| Authentication tokens | Google or Apple | Verifying your identity |

We do not collect or store your Google or Apple account passwords.

### 2.2 Subscription and Payment Information

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and Apple's App Store (or Google Play) for payment processing.

| Data | Collected By | Purpose |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Subscription status (active, expired, cancelled) | RevenueCat / Our servers | Determining access to the App |
| Product identifier (plan type) | RevenueCat | Subscription management |
| Purchase receipt validation | Apple App Store / Google Play | Verifying legitimate purchases |
| Original app user ID | RevenueCat | Linking subscription to account |
| Subscription expiration date | RevenueCat / Our servers | Managing subscription lifecycle |

**We do not collect or store your credit card number, billing address, or any direct payment information.** All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play).

### 2.3 Device and Machine Information

| Data | Purpose | Storage |
|------|---------|---------|
| Device ID (random UUID generated by us) | Multi-device support and licensing enforcement | Encrypted on device + hashed on our servers |
| Machine ID (hash only) | Licensing: ensuring the App is used within authorized machine limits | Our servers (hash only, not reversible) |
| Platform type (iOS/Android) | Providing correct app experience | Our servers |
| Device name (generic, e.g., "iPhone") | Multi-device management display | Our servers |

We generate these identifiers ourselves. We do not read your hardware serial numbers, UDID, IDFA, or any Apple/Google advertising identifiers.

### 2.4 Data Stored Locally on Your Device Only

The following data is stored exclusively on your mobile device using platform-native encrypted storage and is never transmitted to our servers:

| Data | Storage Method | Purpose |
|------|---------------|---------|
| Bridge connection credentials (IP, port, auth token) | iOS Keychain / Android EncryptedSharedPreferences | Connecting to your bridge server |
| Cached chat sessions and messages | Encrypted local storage (max 50 messages per session) | Offline viewing |
| Cached project lists | Encrypted local storage | Offline viewing |
| App settings and preferences (theme, notification preferences, selected agent) | Encrypted local storage | Remembering your preferences |
| TLS certificate fingerprints | Encrypted local storage | Secure connection verification |

### 2.5 Data We Do NOT Collect

We want to be explicit about what we never collect, access, or store on our servers:

- **Your source code or files** -- Code stays on your computer; the App acts as a remote interface only
- **Your AI chat conversations or prompts** -- These flow directly between your phone and your computer
- **Your AI session data or history** -- Stored only on your local machine
- **Your API keys or AI provider credentials** -- Configured and stored on your computer only
- **Your location data** -- We never access GPS or location services
- **Your contacts or address book**
- **Your browsing or search history**
- **Health, fitness, or biometric data**
- **Advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID)** -- We do not use any advertising SDKs
- **Usage analytics or behavioral telemetry** -- We do not use analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.)
- **Crash reports** -- We do not use crash reporting services (no Crashlytics, Sentry, Bugsnag, etc.)

## 4. How Your Data Flows

```
Your Phone (Broski App)
        |
        | (1) Auth: Google/Apple Sign-In tokens
        ↓
Our Cloud (Convex) ←── (2) RevenueCat subscription webhooks
  Stores: email, subscription status,
  machine ID hashes, session tokens
        |
        | (3) Session token returned to app
        ↓
Your Phone (Broski App)
        |
        | (4) Direct peer-to-peer connection
        |     (Local WiFi, Tailscale VPN, or Tailscale Funnel)
        ↓
Your Computer (Bridge Server)
        |
        | (5) Local process communication
        ↓
AI Agents (Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex)
        |
        | (6) API calls using YOUR configured keys
        ↓
AI Providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, etc.)
```

**Steps 4-6 do not involve our servers.** Your code, conversations, and AI interactions flow directly between your devices and the AI providers you configure.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

### 6.1 On Your Mobile Device

All locally stored data uses platform-native encrypted storage:
- **iOS:** Keychain Services with hardware-backed encryption
- **Android:** EncryptedSharedPreferences with AES-256-GCM encryption

### 6.2 On Our Servers (Convex Cloud)

- Account data is stored on Convex's infrastructure (hosted in the United States)
- Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest
- Access is restricted to authenticated requests only
- Machine IDs are stored as irreversible hashes

### 6.3 Network Security

| Connection Type | Encryption | Exposure |
|----------------|-----------|----------|
| App to our cloud (auth/subscription) | TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS) | Internet |
| App to your bridge (Local WiFi) | WPA2/WPA3 network-level | Local network only |
| App to your bridge (Tailscale VPN) | WireGuard (ChaCha20-Poly1305, end-to-end) | Your Tailnet only |
| App to your bridge (Tailscale Funnel) | TLS 1.3 | Public internet (auth token required) |

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

### 8.1 All Users

You have the right to:

- **Access your data:** View your account information within the App (Settings > Account)
- **Delete your account:** Permanently delete your account and all associated data from our servers (Settings > Account > Delete Account)
- **Revoke machines:** Remove registered machines from your account
- **Clear local data:** Clear all cached data from your device (Settings > Clear Cache)
- **Disconnect:** Remove bridge connection and all local session data
- **Uninstall:** Remove the App and all locally stored data from your device
