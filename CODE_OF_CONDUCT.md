# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Broski a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Focusing on what is best for the codebase and users
- Giving and accepting constructive technical feedback respectfully
- Showing empathy and patience toward other contributors
- Keeping discussions grounded in code, reproducible behavior, and clear evidence

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional technical community

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct, or ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to **broskiappp@gmail.com**.
